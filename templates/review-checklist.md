# Review Checklist

## Intent

- Does the change solve the stated problem?
- Does it preserve the surrounding system's assumptions?

## Correctness

- Are happy paths, failure paths, and edge cases covered?
- Are tests meaningful rather than merely present?

## Maintainability

- Is the design understandable to the next maintainer?
- Does the change add unnecessary coupling or complexity?

## Operations and safety

- Are permissions, data handling, and rollback clear?
- Are logs and errors useful without exposing secrets?
